Every reef keeper has a “while I was out” story

  • It’s 2 p.m., you’re stuck in a meeting, and the internet at home has been down for an hour. You can’t see the tank from here. The question is whether your controller still knows to cut the heater and stop the top-off, or whether it’s waiting for the internet to come back.

  • The float switch sticks, and the top-off pump keeps going. Fresh water keeps going into the sump while you sleep, and you wake up to the sound of water running over and onto the floor. The salinity drops, and the mess is in your living room.

  • It’s day two of the trip and you’re at dinner when your phone buzzes: low temperature, Tank. The heater has quit. You text your neighbor, who knows where the spare heater is under the stand. The alert gave you time to sort it out from the restaurant.

Do you want a controller that quits when the internet does, or one that runs locally and keeps going?

Local control, the same idea Home Assistant is built on. The brains are in the box under your stand, so no one’s server has to say yes before it cuts the heater or stops the top-off.

Stuck floats, stuck heaters, wet floors: what the controller does

  1. 01

    Evaporation

    Water level drops and salinity climbs.

    Auto top-off with an overfill lockout.

  2. 02

    Heater failure

    A heater can stick on and overheat the tank, or quit and let it cool.

    Temperature probes switch the heater off above your limit, and alert you if the tank cools.

  3. 03

    Water on the floor

    A leak under the stand goes unseen.

    A leak sensor under the stand alerts you and locks out top-off.

Control Freak’s own safety rules (heater cutoff, top-off lockout and leak shutoff) keep running on the controller, even if Home Assistant stops.

One app for the tank and the house

Home Assistant is built in, so the same app that watches your tank can look after the rest of your home. You get taken care of as well as your fish tank is.

  • One place

    With Home Assistant and the Control Freak integration pre-installed, your tank and compatible smart home devices meet in one app.

  • Works locally

    Home Assistant stays on your home network, and the controller keeps running the tank when the internet connection drops.

  • Open foundation

    Home Assistant is open source, run by a nonprofit, and runs locally, so no company can switch it off. Control Freak adds no subscription to your reef controller.

  • Voice access

    Ask Alexa for tank status, tell Google to adjust the temperature, and hear alerts out loud through Alexa or Google Home.

  • Connect what you already own.

    Home Assistant works with over a thousand brands of smart devices, over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee and Z-Wave. Zigbee and Z-Wave need a plug-in radio.
