How it all connects

One controller under the stand.

The probes, top-off, leak sensor and doser plug into the controller; the pumps, heater and lights plug into the Power Hub. Keyed, colour-coded plugs only fit their own port — scan the QR code and you’re set up.

  1. Temperature probesTwo in the kit, room for more. The heater stands down above your limit.
  2. pH probe (Pro)pH right on the home screen, with trends.
  3. Top-off sensors and pumpTops up evaporation with an overfill lockout. Just add a reservoir.
  4. Leak sensorWater on the floor under the stand is caught early.
  5. 3-head doser (Pro)Ca, Alk and Mg in small, steady doses.
  6. Power Hub8 smart outlets. Return and powerheads stay on if the controller loses power.
  7. Home Assistant, pre-installedOne app for the reef and the home. Voice through Alexa and Google; alerts when you’re away.
