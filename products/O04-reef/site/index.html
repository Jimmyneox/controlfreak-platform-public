Problems the tank throws at you, and what the controller does.

  1. 01

    Evaporation

    Water level drops and salinity climbs.

    Auto top-off with an overfill lockout.

  2. 02

    Heater failure

    A heater can stick on and overheat the tank, or quit and let it cool.

    Temperature probes switch the heater off above your limit, and alert you if the tank cools.

  3. 03

    Water on the floor

    A leak under the stand goes unseen.

    A leak sensor under the stand alerts you and locks out top-off.

Control Freak’s own safety rules — heater cutoff, top-off lockout and leak shutoff — keep running on the controller, even if Home Assistant stops.

Your tank and home, together

  • One place

    With Home Assistant and the Control Freak integration pre-installed, your tank and compatible smart home devices meet in one app.

  • Works locally

    Home Assistant stays on your home network, and the controller keeps running the tank when the internet connection drops.

  • Open foundation

    Home Assistant is open source and run by a nonprofit. Control Freak adds no subscription to your reef controller.

  • Voice access

    Ask Alexa for tank status, tell Google to adjust the temperature, and hear alerts out loud through Alexa or Google Home.
