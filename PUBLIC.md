1. **No App Store app internals** — private APIs, unpublished store listing copy, internal builds, keys, review notes stay out.
2. **No private Platform planning** — `DECISIONS.md`, board pinouts, flush docs, COGS/BOM with costs, and other content from private `Jimmyneox/controlfreak-platform` stay in that private repo.
3. **OK here** — marketing-safe product site/demo code, public landing copy already cleared for launch, industry-specific visual systems that don’t embed private engineering, and specs already handed to Design as marketing-safe.
4. When in doubt, ask James or Chief before committing. Prefer pushing private work to `controlfreak-platform` and only promoting scrubbed artifacts here.
