Smart controllers that run locally, work together in one app, and keep the control with the people who care most.

Our story

Control Freak started in pools, where the big systems charged a premium for boards you couldn't change. We built one that put control back in the owner's hands. Now the same controller runs reef tanks, basements, terrariums and cold plunges.

We're not the control freaks. You are.

What every Control Freak does for you

  1. Local control

    The brains are in the controller, not on someone's server.

  2. One app

    Home Assistant built in, for the whole home.

  3. No subscription.

  4. Safety rules built in

    They keep working when the internet doesn't.

  5. One-tap routines

    Spa Night on the pool, Feed Mode on the reef. One tap runs several steps, then hands control back. More than an on/off switch.

  6. Plug and play kit

    Keyed plugs fit one way.
